    MMORPG skill effects in C++: stone, ice, lightning and fire

    Four new skills in our C++ MMORPG, with shards and spikes that are lit like the world, lightning that strikes again and again, a crescent of fire, and a slice in front of the player for the server to hit.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG gets four new skills, each a move of its own and a list of fx in the skills file. The raylib client draws shards thrown up from the ground and spikes of stone and ice that come up nearest first, as solid gems lit by the world's shader, then lightning that finds a new path each flicker, and a crescent of fire that flies forward from the player. The C++ server gets an arc, so a skill can hit a slice in front of the player and nothing behind. Earthsplitter, Frost Nova, Searing Arc and Stormcaller are entries in a text file, on top of last episode's moves and fx.
