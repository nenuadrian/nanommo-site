    MMORPG drag and drop in C++ for the inventory and the hotbar

    Our C++ MMORPG's hotbar, chat, bag and login screens get the new look, and items and skills drag for real, checked by the server's own rules.

    Watch on YouTube

    The second half of our MMORPG's new interface, in C++ with Dear ImGui and raylib. The hotbar gets slots of brass in two rows, and a skill dragged onto another swaps with it, in an order the settings keep. The chat closes into a button that counts the lines since. Items drag as themselves, lit green or red by the server's own inventory rules, which the client now links as a library, and a move shows at once, before the server answers, and goes back if it says no. The character window becomes a paper doll, an item dropped over the world asks to be thrown away, and the login, the worlds, the characters, the loading and the death screens get the look. This is episode 64 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
