Procedural terrain in C++ with noise and raylib

Our board of tiles becomes an island 512 metres across, from noise and a seed that the server and the client share, with a camera that follows you.

We replace our board of tiles with a procedural island, 512 metres across, from value noise and a seed that the server and the client share. Both have to compute the island to the last bit, so we turn off fused multiply-adds and pin a fingerprint in a test, and the client draws it low-poly, with a sea, fog and a camera that follows you.
