    raylib 3D animation in C++: Mixamo and glTF

    Mixamo characters converted to glTF, merged into files raylib can load, synced through the server and skinned on the GPU.

    Watch on YouTube

    We turn our cubes into 3D characters with skeletal animation in raylib, from Mixamo's X Bot and Y Bot, converted from FBX to glTF in Docker. Loading them as they come doesn't work, so a small glTF tool fixes their skins and bones, and the client blends the clips and skins the characters on the GPU. This is episode 7 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
