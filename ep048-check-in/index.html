Recap and what's next

A check-in after forty-seven episodes, on what we've built, the practices around it, and where the game goes next.

Watch on YouTube

A check-in, with no code this time: a look back over the forty-seven episodes so far, from a C++ server and a raylib client to quests, a load test with Pyroscope and analytics in ClickHouse. We go over how the game fits together today, play it, and look at the tools around it, the dashboards and CI, and the practices from the industry we've put in place. Then the plan: more of the features you'd expect in an MMORPG, more worlds and graphics, a trading system, all the players simulated by bots, and more on security. This is episode 48 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
