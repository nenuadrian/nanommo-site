    A data-driven item system for an MMO game server

    Items made of parts instead of types, with stacks, equipment slots, stats, potions and rolls, and a bag to carry them in, which a property test plays with twenty thousand times.

    Watch on YouTube

    We design the item system of our MMORPG as data: an item is the parts it has, such as how many stack, where it's worn and what it adds, what using one does and what each one rolls, instead of a type in the code, and the content's checks refuse parts that don't go together. Then a bag of twenty-four slots, with a slot for each piece of equipment, which stacks, splits, swaps and wears items, and a property test that makes twenty thousand random moves and checks that no item is ever made or lost. This is episode 39 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
