    The game we're building from scratch in C++, filmed by itself, every skill slowed at its hit.

    nanoMMO is an MMORPG we're building from scratch in C++, client and server, one episode at a time. This is its trailer, filmed by the game itself: the client's own renderer draws every shot frame by frame, so the game's clock can run slower than the film's, and the slow motion, the sky and the sound are the game's. A series about building an MMORPG from scratch, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
