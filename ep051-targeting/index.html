    MMORPG click targeting in C++ to pick a monster, run up and attack

    Swings and skills that go for the monster you clicked, with a ring at its feet and a frame of its own, a run up to it when it's out of reach, and cursors over what a click would do, in our C++ MMORPG.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG's fights go for the monster you pick now, not whatever's nearest. The C++ server keeps each player's pick from their input, and swings and skills that hit one monster go for it. In the raylib client, a click on a monster picks it, with a red ring at its feet and a frame beside yours, and the hotbar shows which skills need a pick. The cursor turns into a hand over someone you can talk to, and a crosshair over a monster, and an NPC answers a click only from within talking reach. And a swing or a skill at a monster out of reach runs you up to it first, on the server. This is episode 51 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
