    MMORPG sound effects in C++: footsteps made from code

    Footsteps for our C++ MMORPG, which the client makes from numbers with a small synthesizer of our own, for each kind of ground, timed to the frames where the walk puts each foot down.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG gets its first sound effects, and the raylib client makes them from numbers as it starts, without a single sound file. We build a small synthesizer in C++: noise, a state variable filter, sine waves and envelopes. From those come footsteps for grass, sand, rock, snow, ash, stone and water, in takes that don't repeat back to back, timed to the frames where the walk puts each foot down, and heard by distance and side. A small tool of our own plays each sound outside the game. This is episode 55 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
