    Checking game content as a graph for an indie MMO

    Our MMO's content as a graph in smollerGraph, the graph database from the other series, and a test that fails when a new player can't reach a quest, a monster, an item or a skill, run in CI.

    Watch on YouTube

    Checking an indie MMO's game content as a graph, so the build fails when a new player can't reach something. Our content links a quest to the NPC who gives it, a monster to what it drops, a land to the one behind its gate, and our checks made sure each link pointed at something real. Taking the Guard out of the Meadows passed all of them, while nobody could take his quest anymore. So the content goes into smollerGraph, the graph database from the other series on this channel, fetched with CMake at a version, and a test asks it, in Cypher, for a shortest path from a new player to each land, monster, NPC, quest, item and skill. Its explorer draws the whole game, and the test runs in CI with the others. This is episode 90 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
