    MMORPG pathfinding in C++ with A*, a map and a minimap

    A* pathfinding over the island's walkable squares on the C++ server, click to walk there, a map and a minimap drawn from the land, and monster camps spread over it, in our MMORPG.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG gets pathfinding. The C++ server marks each metre square of a land a player can walk on, and finds the way around trees, lakes and lava with A*: a priority queue ordered by the cost so far plus the octile distance left, a cap on how much it searches, and a way straightened to the places where it turns. A search across the island takes about a quarter of a millisecond. A double click on the ground, or a click on the new map, walks you there by yourself. The raylib client draws each land from above into a texture for the map on M and the minimap in the corner, and the monster camps spread over the land, at least thirty metres apart. This is episode 52 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
