C++ sanitizers, fuzzing and coverage in Docker

Sanitizers under the bots, fuzzing, clang-tidy and a coverage report find what the tests don't, and we fix it.

Watch on YouTube

We look for the bugs our tests don't find, with AddressSanitizer, UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer and ThreadSanitizer while two hundred bots play, a fuzzer, clang-tidy and a coverage report. They find a leak in raylib, a data race in our metrics' web server and stories the storyteller would lose, and we fix what they find, in Docker and in CI. This is episode 28 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
