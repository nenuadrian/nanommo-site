    Weather effects and ambient sound in a C++ MMORPG

    Our C++ MMORPG's film tool shoots the weather over a player's shoulder, while fire falls on the lava, fireflies come out after dusk and the rain, the thunder and the crickets get their sound, first made from numbers and then, for the rain and the fire, recorded.

    Watch on YouTube

    Fire rain, snow, fireflies and the sound of the weather in our MMORPG, in C++ with raylib. The film tool gets the sky the server would send and a camera over a player's shoulder, and films thirteen quiet shots of the lands. Rain turns to snow above the snow line, ash and fire fall over the Ashlands' lava, the fire glowing past white so it blooms, and fireflies, wisps and lava sparks live in cells of the ground, so they stay put as the camera moves. The weather's sound is made from numbers, in loops folded so they go round without a seam and fed to an audio stream a buffer at a time, with thunder that comes after the flash at the speed of sound. The rain made from numbers sounded like glass beads, so the rain and the fire become recordings, kept out of git. This is episode 86 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
