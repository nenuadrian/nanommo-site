    Game character screen in C++ with raylib, ImGui

    A character screen with our player's details next to an idle 3D preview, which a camera of its own draws into a render texture for Dear ImGui to show.

    Watch on YouTube

    We build a character screen with your name, level, health, experience and position, next to an idle model of your character. The client draws the model into a render texture through a camera of its own, Dear ImGui shows it in a window, and a test checks the preview on a hidden window. This is episode 17 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
