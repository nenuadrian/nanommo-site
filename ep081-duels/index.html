    MMORPG PvP duels in C++ that end at one health

    Two players in our C++ MMORPG agree to a duel, count down from three, pick each other as they would a monster, and fight until one is left with one health, with the server deciding each step.

    Watch on YouTube

    Player versus player duels for our MMORPG, in C++. /duel and a name asks a player within ten metres, and once they say yes, in the chat or on the question on their screen, a flag goes down and the server counts three seconds down on both screens. Then each picks the other, as they would a monster, in a field of its own beside the monster's, which the world keeps for the opponent and nobody else, and swings and skills land on them. The blow that would leave a duellist without health leaves them one, and they've lost, as has anyone who goes more than 25 metres from the flag: nobody dies in a duel. The world keeps the duels, the server sends both sides each change over TCP, and the raylib client draws the countdown, the flag and a ring at the opponent's feet. This is episode 81 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
