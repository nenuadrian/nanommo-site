    Prometheus Alertmanager: alert rules and email

    Prometheus alerts when a world falls behind or goes down, Alertmanager emails each alert to a mail server in Docker, and promtool tests the rules.

    Watch on YouTube

A crashed world drops out of Consul and lets go of its alert, so we write a rule that holds on, and Alertmanager emails each alert to Mailpit, with promtool testing the rules in CI.
