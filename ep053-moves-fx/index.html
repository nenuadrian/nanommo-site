    MMORPG skill animation and VFX in C++ with keyframes, trails and sparks

    Skill animations of our own, as keyframes of joint turns in a text file, with sword trails, glows, shockwaves, sparks and a camera shake, in the raylib client of our C++ MMORPG, and the swing on Space as a skill too.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG's skills move the way we write them now, not the way a downloaded clip does. A skill's move is a few keys in the content, each turning a handful of joints at a time into the skill, with an ease that makes a strike snap and a wind-up gather. The raylib client turns the bones on top of the standing clip, each joint taking the bones below it round, and works out the GPU's skinning matrices again. Then come the fx: a trail where the sword has just been, posed again at moments just gone, glows, rings along the ground, sparks, motes and a camera shake, each worked out from the time since the skill started, so it looks alike at any frame rate. The C++ server sends the monster a skill lands on, and the swing on Space becomes Strike, a skill every player has, with the weapon's reach, damage and timing. This is episode 53 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
