    Drag and drop MMORPG inventory with Dear ImGui

    The server sends each player their bag and takes what they do with it, and the client draws it, with icons, tooltips, drag and drop, and the slots that are worn.

    Watch on YouTube

    We put our MMORPG's inventory on screen.
