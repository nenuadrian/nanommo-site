    MMORPG sound effects in C++: skills and gates from code

    Each skill in our C++ MMORPG gets a sound made from the fx it already lists, from a whoosh of the blade to thunder, and each land a chord for when we arrive.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG's skills get their sounds, made by the raylib client from numbers, without a single sound file. Each kind of fx a skill lists, from a trail to lightning, has a sound of its own, built from noise, tones and filters, with a cold colour ringing higher than a warm one, so a new skill built from them comes with its sound. Going through a gate rises two octaves as the world fades, and each land greets us with a chord: major, minor or diminished. This is episode 56 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
