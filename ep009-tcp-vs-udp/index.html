    TCP vs UDP for a game server, under packet loss

    We make the network drop packets, measure how old the world on a player's screen gets over TCP and then over UDP, and let the numbers decide.

    Watch on YouTube

    We compare TCP and UDP for our game server under packet loss, with netem dropping a share of the packets and our bots measuring how old their newest world is. Over TCP, head-of-line blocking makes the worlds wait for each lost packet, so we send the world over UDP, in parts that fit in a packet, and make that the default. This is episode 9 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
