    MySQL schema migrations with Flyway in Docker

    Flyway keeps our MySQL tables in versions, so a change to them keeps the players' accounts, starting with saving their health.

    Watch on YouTube

    We bring in Flyway to keep our MySQL tables in versioned migrations, so that changing a table no longer means deleting the database and its accounts. Docker Compose runs it before the servers, and then we save the players' health, where an edited first migration fails its checksum and the change gets a second migration instead. This is episode 18 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
