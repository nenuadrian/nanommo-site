    MMORPG sword skills in C++ that dash, launch and juggle

    Five sword skills for our C++ MMORPG, with dashes the server walks the player along, blows that throw monsters into the air and keep them there, copies left behind, a flash, and a camera that goes in.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our MMORPG's sword gets five new skills. The C++ server can now move a player during a skill, along strides in the skills file, so that everyone sees a dash, and a blow can throw a monster into the air, keep it there with the next one and slam it down, for two seconds at most however many players take turns. Each monster's height goes to the raylib client, which tumbles it in the air, draws see-through copies of a player on a fast move, flashes the screen of the player using a skill and takes their camera in. Phantom Rush, Skyrend, Tempest Flurry, Cyclone and Severing Moon are entries in a text file, on a hotbar with a second row on Shift. This is episode 62 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
