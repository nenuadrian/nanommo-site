    Instanced dungeons for an MMORPG game server

    The Crypt, a dungeon that gives each player who goes in a copy of it of their own, with copies that go once they're empty, and players saved outside it.

    Watch on YouTube

    We give each player who walks into a dungeon a copy of it of their own, the way MMORPGs instance their dungeons. The world server runs its lands as copies, the gates get a list of their own, and the Crypt, our first dungeon, gets a copy for each player who goes in, with its own skeletons. The nanommo_copies metric shows that the copies stay after forty bots leave, so a copy nobody's in stops, and goes after five minutes, and players in the Crypt are saved outside it, so that a logout or a deploy brings them back to the Meadows. This is episode 38 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
