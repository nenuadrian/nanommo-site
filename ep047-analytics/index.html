MMORPG game analytics with ClickHouse and Grafana

What players do, as events, from the C++ server through a Redis stream into ClickHouse, a column-oriented database, and onto a Grafana dashboard of the game.

Watch on YouTube

We add game analytics to our MMORPG: each kill, drop, quest taken and turned in, level reached, potion drunk and death is an event, recorded by the world on the C++ server. Once a tick, the server adds them to a Redis stream, so the game never waits on the analytics, and a recorder takes them from there, as a consumer group, in batches, into ClickHouse, a column-oriented database that counts millions of rows quickly. Then a Grafana dashboard of the game, from ClickHouse: kills a minute, the quests taken against the ones done, the items that come into the game against the ones used, and where players die. This is episode 47 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
