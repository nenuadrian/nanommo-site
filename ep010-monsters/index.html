C++ MMO monsters: AI, health and levels

A public-domain skeleton, monsters with an AI on the server, and health, levels and experience for everyone.

Watch on YouTube

We add the first monsters to our world, a skeleton from Quaternius that the client loads as it is, with a wander AI that the server runs each tick. Players and monsters get health and levels, and players get experience, saved in MySQL and shown with health bars over everyone. This is episode 10 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
