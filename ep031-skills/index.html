    MMORPG skills, cooldowns and a hotbar in C++

    Four skills as content, used on the server with a level and a cooldown each, a hotbar on the number keys, animations from Mixamo, and a camera that tilts.

This is episode 31 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
