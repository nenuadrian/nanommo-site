MMORPG loot tables and drops on a C++ server

Monsters drop loot from tables in the content, each item with its chance, into the killer's bag, the chat says what dropped, and the bots drink and dress.

Watch on YouTube

We give our MMORPG's monsters loot tables. Each kind of monster lists the items it can drop, each with a chance and how many, and when it dies, the server rolls them and puts what drops into its killer's bag, a stack at a time or one at a time with rolls of their own, and the chat says what dropped, or what didn't fit. The content's checks refuse loot that doesn't add up, the bots drink potions and wear what they find, and the world fuzzer moves items to any place a client could send. This is episode 42 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
