    Saving MMO inventories with Redis and MySQL

    Each player carries a bag and fights with what they wear, drinks potions with a cooldown, and has their bag saved, through Redis to MySQL, when it changes.

    Watch on YouTube

    We give each player of our MMORPG a bag to carry, and save it. A new player gets their kit once, what they wear adds to their damage, health and armour, and a potion from the bag heals them, with a cooldown of its own. Then the bags are saved through Redis to MySQL, a row for each stack, only when they change, in a transaction, and in statements small enough for MySQL to take five hundred full bags at once. This is episode 40 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
