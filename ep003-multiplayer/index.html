    TCP, for now

    2:44 For the client and the server to talk, we have to pick between the two ways the internet gives us to send data, TCP and UDP. TCP gives us a connection, and a stream of bytes that arrives reliably and in order. If a packet gets lost on the way, TCP sends it again, and the operating system takes care of all of that for us. The catch is that the bytes after a lost packet wait until it's been resent, even when they've arrived already.
