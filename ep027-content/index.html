    Data-driven MMO content in C++ with protobuf

    Monsters and NPCs join the items as data both sides read, go out by kind, and a content hash keeps clients and worlds in step.

    Watch on YouTube

    We turn the rest of our game's content into data, with the kinds of monsters and NPCs, and where they stand, in a content folder that both sides read. Monsters and NPCs go out by their kind's id, a hash of the content keeps clients off worlds they don't match, and a new monster joins without a line of code. This is episode 27 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
