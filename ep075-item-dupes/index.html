    How MMO item dupes happen

    Our C++ MMORPG's red-team client learns to trade, and twice keeps the sword it gives away, until the server holds what's offered where it is, checks it before it gives anything, and saves both bags of a trade as one.

    Watch on YouTube

    How item dupes happen in an MMORPG, and how the server stops them, in C++. Our red-team client learns to trade, with a partner that keeps the rules, and finds two dupes in last episode's simple trading. swap offers its sword, and once the partner's ready, moves it to another slot: the trade gives the copy it made at the offer and takes nothing, so two swords become three. run says it's ready and leaves at once, before the next tick saves the bags, and comes back to its old bag, sword and all. Now the server holds what's offered where it is, checks each place before it gives anything, has a ready name the revision of the trade it's for, and saves both players of a trade at once, in one Redis transaction with MULTI and EXEC, and a player as they leave. This is episode 75 of a series about building an MMORPG from scratch in C++, with the focus on the engineering around the game.
